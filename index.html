FAQ
What is SugarBreak?
SugarBreak is an AI coach that helps you quit added sugar. When a craving hits, it talks you through it in real time and reminds you why you started. It scans your food for hidden sugar, keeps a private vault of your motivation, and tracks your sugar-free streak. The food scanner is free and no sign-up is needed to start.
How does the craving rescue work?
When you feel a craving, you open the SOS. It starts a guided breathing exercise and a 10-minute timer — cravings usually peak and pass within that window. The coach talks to you the whole time, surfaces your saved reasons and voice notes, and helps you ride it out without breaking your streak.
Is this medical advice?
No. SugarBreak is a wellness and habit-change app, not a medical device. Its sugar estimates are informational, not clinical. Always consult your doctor before making dietary changes, especially if you have diabetes or another health condition.
How much does it cost?
The sugar scanner is free. The AI coach comes with a 7-day free trial, no credit card required. After that, Premium unlocks unlimited coaching, craving rescue and daily briefings. Pricing varies by country and is shown in your local currency in the app — cancel anytime.
What is the best app to quit sugar?
The best sugar-quitting app is one that helps you through the actual craving, not just tracks what you ate afterward. SugarBreak combines a real-time craving SOS, an AI coach that references your own streak and reasons, and a free food scanner — features most calorie or habit trackers don't offer together. It's built specifically around the moment a craving hits, which is when most quit-sugar attempts fail.
What happens if I slip and eat sugar?
You start again — and the coach helps you do it without guilt. A slip resets your streak honestly, but the app treats it as data, not failure. The point is the long-term habit change, and one bad day doesn't undo your progress.
Do I need an account?
No. SugarBreak works with no sign-up. Your streak, check-ins and motivation vault are stored on your own device, so your journey stays private.
